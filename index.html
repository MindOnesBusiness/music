The big idea

Music is a color wheel you can hear

Painters use a color wheel. Musicians have one too. It is called the circle of fifths. Put the two together, and music gets much easier to see.

LESSON 1

Notes are colors

Music has 12 notes. We give each note its own color. Now you can see a song, not just hear it.

LESSON 2

Scales are palettes

A scale is a set of 7 notes. On the wheel, a happy scale is 7 colors in a row. Like a paint set that always matches.

LESSON 3

Chords are color sets

A chord is 3 or more notes played at once. Close colors make a calm chord. Far colors make a bold one.

On the board

What we will learn

  1. The 12 notes. Learn each note by its color and sound.
  2. Rhythm and beat. Feel the pulse that holds a song together.
  3. Scales and keys. Find the color "family" a song lives in.
  4. Chords that work. Build chords and see why they sound good.
  5. Write your own song. Paint with sound and make something new.
